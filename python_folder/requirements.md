The focus is on students' frequency of access, time spent, and interactions with learning materials in Blackboard. The aim will be to explore patterns of student engagement with Blackboard materials, focusing on the frequency of access, time spent on course materials, types of content accessed, students' characteristics and scores.
Frequency of students engagement with materials on Blackboard each week
Types of materials (e.g., learning materials, reading list, assignments, module handbook) do students access
Time students spend on Blackboard materials each week and impact on their performance
Factors (e.g.student characteristics) influence the frequency and duration of student engagement
Relationship between student demographics (e.g., age, gender, experience, academic performance) and their engagement patterns.
Time management: Variations in time spent on LMS platforms may relate to different student time management skills (e.g. a) Proactivity: access materials a week in advance, b) regularity: accessing blackboard material every week, c) revision strategies: revisit previous weeks materials.
