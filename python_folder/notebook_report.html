# Prepare data for survival analysis (Week 1)
day_cols = ['monday', 'tuesday', 'wednesday', 'thursday', 'friday', 'saturday', 'sunday']
df_survival = df1[['student_id', 'total_marks'] + day_cols].copy()
df_survival['event'] = (df_survival['total_marks'] >= 70).astype(int)

# Fit Kaplan-Meier estimator
kmf = KaplanMeierFitter()
kmf.fit(durations=df_survival['duration'], event_observed=df_survival['event'])

plt.figure(figsize=(8, 5))
kmf.plot_survival_function()
plt.title('Survival Analysis: Days of Activity vs. Probability of Achieving ≥70 Marks (Week 1)')
plt.xlabel('Number of Active Days in Week 1')
plt.ylabel('Survival Probability (Not Achieving ≥70 Marks)')
plt.grid(True)

Insight:

The Kaplan-Meier survival analysis shows that students who are active on more days during Week 3 have a higher probability of achieving a distinction (total_marks ≥ 70).
